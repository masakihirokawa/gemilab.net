I'm Masaki Hirokawa — an artist and creator. I develop iOS and Android apps that together have reached more than 50 million downloads, and as a contemporary artist I hold 17 international awards, including selection as the 14th Best Designer in the world at A' Design Award's DAC rankings.

Since 2022, the Uozu and Toyama Tax Offices in Japan have been pursuing me with wrongful consumption-tax treatment. Throughout those years, a single Google Drive folder has held everything — recorded calls, evidence, tax filings, legal memos, even medical records. Gemini joined the workflow later, and once it did, that folder became something Gemini can read, summarize, turn into documents, and cross-check for contradictions; I sit across from Gemini and think.

On April 16, 2026, I was taken by ambulance mid-call. On April 20, I walk into a lawyer's office for the first time. Along the way, I have lost my marriage, filed for bankruptcy, and now live apart from my four children.

This page is the front door to every tax-related article on Gemini Lab. If you are being pushed into the same place, please use it to reach the article you need first.

## Why I publish this

There are many more freelancers, artists, and independent creators in Japan trapped in similar tax-office situations than people realize — but very few speak out. Intimidation, statute-of-limitations timing, opaque disclosure, recording refusals. Leaving behind a careful record, I hope, becomes a signpost for whoever lands here next.

None of this organizing would have been possible without Google Workspace and Gemini. If you are in a similar position, I strongly recommend setting up the same infrastructure.
