## Ask a Tax Official for the Legal Basis — They Cannot Answer. Gemini Can.

During tax filing season in Japan, calling a tax office typically ends with "Please consult a tax advisor." Even when you cite specific legal provisions, the official on the other end frequently cannot engage with the substance of your question.

Ask Google's Gemini the same question, and it returns the relevant statutory provisions, enforcement orders, administrative circulars, and even related case law — organized and consistent regardless of how you phrase the query.

This is not a personal grievance. It is a structural problem documented extensively in Claude Lab's [Fighting Unfair Taxation with AI](https://claudelab.net/articles/claude-ai/fighting-unfair-taxation-with-ai), which reveals systemic issues in Japan's tax administration that affect countless taxpayers.

## Why AI Delivers More Accurate Tax Judgments

### Comprehensive Knowledge Coverage

Tax advisors specialize, and no single advisor masters every area of tax law. Digital advertising revenue (AdMob, AdSense) represents a relatively new business model that many tax professionals in Japan have limited experience with.

Gemini cross-references the Consumption Tax Act, Income Tax Act, Corporate Tax Act, tax treaties, and administrative circulars simultaneously. For a question about AdMob revenue classification, it synthesizes Article 7 (export exemption), the special provisions for "electronically provided services," and the reverse charge mechanism — all in a single, coherent response.

### Computational Precision

Gemini's strength lies in its foundation on Google's infrastructure, making it particularly capable at processing and validating large volumes of numerical data. You can feed it thousands of transaction rows in CSV format and have it verify the consumption tax classification for each line.

```
Example prompt:
"Please verify the consumption tax classification for each
transaction in this CSV data, paying special attention to:
- Whether revenue from overseas entities (Google Asia Pacific,
  Apple Distribution International) qualifies as export-exempt
  or non-taxable
- Domestic transaction taxable/non-taxable determination
- Identification of expenses eligible for input tax credits

Transaction data:
[paste CSV data]"
```

Human tax advisors have finite time per client. During peak filing season, some resort to sampling rather than reviewing every transaction. Gemini has no such constraint.

## Beyond Tax Offices — The Entire Public Service System Needs Reform

This problem cannot be confined to tax administration alone. Municipal government offices across Japan — city halls, ward offices — exhibit the same structural failures in their public-facing services.

Resident tax reductions, national health insurance premium waivers, childcare support applications — these are programs that directly impact citizens' daily lives. Yet whether a citizen actually learns about and accesses these programs depends on the knowledge and attitude of whichever staff member happens to be at the window.

The root cause is the absence of service quality measurement in public administration. Private companies routinely conduct customer satisfaction surveys, track Net Promoter Scores, and monitor review ratings. Government agencies have virtually no such feedback loops. Public servants draw their salaries from taxpayer money, but the institutional design includes no mechanism to ensure the quality of service provided to those same taxpayers.

AI-powered service windows offer a practical solution. An AI trained on laws, ordinances, and internal regulations handles first-line inquiries, escalating only complex cases to human specialists. This single change would dramatically improve the consistency and accuracy of service that citizens receive.

### Step 1: Map Your Tax Landscape

Begin by having Gemini organize the complete tax picture for your business type.

```

- App Store sales (Apple Distribution International)
- Domestic app development contracts

Please organize systematically: required filing forms,

income tax calculation methodology."
```

Gemini will list the required documents (Final Tax Return Form B, Consumption Tax Return, Blue Return Financial Statements) and explain how to complete each section.

### Step 2: Leverage Google Workspace Integration

One of Gemini's key advantages is integration with Google Workspace. Organize your transaction data in Google Sheets, then ask Gemini to verify formulas, validate aggregations, and check for classification errors directly within your spreadsheet workflow.

### Step 3: Year-over-Year Comparative Analysis

Feed Gemini your past three years of tax return data for comparative analysis. Items showing sharp fluctuations carry higher audit risk, so preparing clear explanations for these variances in advance is essential for audit preparedness.

## Other Countries Are Already Moving

Estonia's e-government platform delivers 99% of public services online. Only three procedures — birth registration, marriage, and real estate transactions — still require in-person interaction. Everything else is digital, with AI chatbots handling citizen inquiries around the clock.

Singapore's IRAS (Inland Revenue Authority) has deployed AI-assisted tax filing that reduces average individual filing time to 15 minutes. South Korea's NTS (National Tax Service) operates nationwide AI tax consultation services.

Japan established its Digital Agency several years ago, but AI transformation of frontline government services has barely begun. My Number Card adoption has increased, but the services built on top of it remain at the "digitizing paper forms" stage — a far cry from genuine AI-powered public administration.

## AI Is a Tool for Better Government

AI transformation of government service windows is not about eliminating public sector jobs. When AI handles routine inquiries, human officials can focus on what they should be doing: resolving complex cases and providing the empathetic, nuanced support that genuinely requires a human touch.

The current reality is that routine inquiries consume so much staff time that complex cases receive inadequate attention, and overall service quality suffers across the board. AI deployment breaks this cycle.

With Gemini, you can start verifying your tax filing with greater accuracy than most human advisors today. You do not need to wait for government reform.
