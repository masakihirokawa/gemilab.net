## The Complete Timeline

### 2014–2020: Export Business Success and Unknowing Overpayment

I operated a mobile app business with Google AdMob as my primary revenue source. AdMob revenue is paid through Google Ireland Ltd — an international transaction that is **export-exempt (non-taxable)** under Japan's Consumption Tax Act.

I unknowingly paid approximately **¥5 million ($33,000) in consumption tax** over these years. Neither my tax accountant nor the tax office ever flagged this.

### 2021: Miyazaki Tax Office Refuses Refund

When I filed a correction request in 2021, the Miyazaki Tax Office **refused** — with no substantive explanation.

### 2021–2024: The Non-Taxable Stalling Tactic

Instead of refunding, they offered to treat future income as non-taxable. This was **a stalling tactic** while the statute of limitations on my refund claim expired.

### 2024: Reverse Billing After Statute Expiration

A 2024 tax audit revealed my refund right had expired. The ¥5 million was gone. Then they **demanded repayment for the non-taxable period** — refuse the refund, wait for expiration, then bill in reverse. Calculated institutional misconduct.

### 2024–Present: Seizure Threats on $300/Month Income

Current reality:

## Legal Analysis

**Good Faith Doctrine Violation**: Offering non-taxable treatment, then reversing it after statute expiration, breaches trust protection (Civil Code Art. 1-2).

**State Liability**: Intimidation, privacy violations, and obstruction of correction requests may trigger claims under Japan's State Liability Act (Art. 1).

**Unlawful Seizure**: Demanding $400/month from someone earning $300/month violates Article 153 of the National Tax Collection Act.

**Privacy Rights**: Interrogating about divorce and relationships violates constitutional privacy rights (Art. 13).

### Tax Accountants and Tax Offices — Too Close for Comfort

Many tax accountants are former tax office employees who maintain close ties with their former colleagues. This creates a structural conflict of interest. Independent organizations like **Minshu Shoko Kai** are essential for genuine taxpayer advocacy.
