Gemini Live lets you have a real conversation with Gemini using your voice — no typing required. It's one of the most impressive features in the Gemini ecosystem, but like any real-time audio feature, it can run into issues.

If your microphone isn't responding, the connection keeps dropping, or audio isn't coming through, this guide will help you identify what's going wrong and fix it.

## Requirements for Gemini Live

Before troubleshooting, let's make sure the basics are in place:

**Supported platforms**:
- Google Gemini app (iOS and Android)
- Browser-based Gemini at gemini.google.com (Chrome recommended)
- Stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or 4G/5G)

**Hardware**:
- Device microphone and speaker access must be allowed
- If using Bluetooth earphones, verify the connection is stable before starting

## Mobile Issues (iOS and Android)

### Issue 1: Microphone Button Doesn't Respond

**Cause**: The app likely doesn't have microphone permission.

**On iOS**:
1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone
2. Find the Gemini or Google app in the list
3. Make sure the toggle is turned on
4. Close and relaunch the app

**On Android**:
1. Settings → Apps → Gemini (or Google) → Permissions
2. Check that Microphone is set to "Allow only while using the app" or "Ask every time"
3. Relaunch the app after changing this

### Issue 2: Connection Drops Mid-Conversation

**Cause**: Network instability or the app moving to the background.

**What to try**:
- Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to see if one is more stable
- If on Wi-Fi, move closer to your router
- Keep the app in the foreground during the conversation — some devices cut background connections
- Update the app to the latest version via the App Store or Google Play

### Issue 3: Your Voice Isn't Being Recognized

**Possible causes**:
- Another app is using the microphone (e.g., a phone call)
- You're speaking too quietly or too briefly
- Background noise is interfering

**What to try**:
- End any active calls before starting Gemini Live
- Speak clearly and at a moderate volume, directly toward your device
- Try using Gemini Live in a quieter environment

## Browser (Desktop) Issues

### Issue 1: "Allow Microphone Access" Prompt

The browser needs explicit permission to use your microphone with Gemini.

**In Chrome**:
1. Click the lock icon 🔒 in the address bar
2. Find "Microphone" and change it to "Allow"
3. Reload the page

**In Safari**:
1. Safari → Settings → Websites → Microphone
2. Set gemini.google.com to "Allow"

### Issue 2: Gemini Seems to Hear You, But No Audio Comes Back

**Check these settings**:
- System volume — is it muted or very low?
- Browser tab — is the speaker icon showing a mute symbol? (Click it to unmute)
- Audio output device — is your browser sending audio to the right speaker or headphone?

### Issue 3: Gemini Live Won't Start While Other Audio Is Playing

Another application or browser tab occupying your audio device can prevent Gemini Live from initializing.

**What to try**:
- Pause any video or music playback in other apps or tabs
- Try opening Gemini in a separate browser window

## Network-Related Problems

Gemini Live requires a more stable connection than regular text chat because it processes audio in real time.

**Quick connection check**:
- Visit [fast.com](https://fast.com) or [speedtest.net](https://www.speedtest.net)
- A connection of at least 10 Mbps download / 5 Mbps upload should be sufficient

**If your connection seems fine but issues persist**:
- Disable your VPN temporarily — VPNs can add latency that disrupts real-time audio
- Corporate or school networks may have firewall rules blocking real-time audio streams; try your home network or mobile data instead

## When It Might Be a Bug

If you've worked through all of the above and nothing has helped, there may be a temporary issue on Google's end or with the app itself.

**Steps to try**:
1. Clear the app cache
   - Android: Settings → Apps → Gemini → Storage → Clear Cache
   - iOS: Delete and reinstall the app
2. Restart your device
3. Try again after a few hours — service-side issues often resolve on their own

**Reporting the issue**:
Use the in-app feedback button ("Send feedback") to report the problem to Google. When many users report the same issue, it gets prioritized for a fix.

## Looking back

Most Gemini Live problems come down to one of three things: microphone permissions, network instability, or another app occupying the audio hardware. Start with permissions (easiest to fix), then check your network, then try a cache clear or reinstall.

With a bit of troubleshooting, you should be able to get Gemini Live working reliably. Once it's running smoothly, it's genuinely a delightful way to interact with Gemini — worth the effort to set up correctly.
