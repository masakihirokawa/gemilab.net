You've set up custom instructions in Gemini Gems, but somehow the AI keeps ignoring them. Sound familiar?

Custom instructions are a powerful way to shape Gemini's behavior to match your preferences and needs, but a few common configuration mistakes or misunderstandings about how they work can cause them to fail silently. This guide breaks down the most common reasons custom instructions don't work as expected and shows you exactly how to fix them—in beginner-friendly language.

## Understanding the Symptoms

First, let's figure out which type of problem you're experiencing. Custom instructions can fail in different ways, and identifying your specific issue is half the battle.

**Pattern 1: Custom instructions set, but completely ignored**

The settings screen says "Saved successfully," yet when you chat with your Gem, the instructions aren't being followed at all. The responses are identical to what you'd get from a Gem with no custom instructions.

**Pattern 2: Instructions work at first, then fade out**

When you start a new conversation, everything looks good—your Gem is honoring your instructions. But as the conversation grows longer, the guidance gradually stops being applied.

**Pattern 3: Instructions work selectively**

Your Gem respects custom instructions for some topics but completely ignores them for others. It's inconsistent and unpredictable.

**Pattern 4: Instructions work for you but not for others**

When you use the Gem yourself, everything runs smoothly. But as soon as you share it with a colleague or friend, they report that custom instructions aren't being applied at all.

## Common Cause #1: How You're Writing the Instructions

The effectiveness of custom instructions depends heavily on their quality and clarity. Gemini might struggle if your instructions are written in certain problematic ways.

**Vague or contradictory instructions**

If you're asking for "friendly responses" and "professional tone" in the same instruction without clarifying the balance, Gemini faces a decision problem. Similarly, instructions like "Answer quickly, but be thorough" create ambiguity that can cause the AI to ignore your guidance entirely.

**Good instruction example:**
"Keep all responses to 200 words or fewer. Always explain any technical terms in parentheses. Use a warm, conversational tone."

**Bad instruction example:**
"Be helpful but concise, giving detailed answers when needed but keeping it brief."

**Instructions that are too long**

Cramming 3,000+ characters into the custom instructions field can overwhelm Gemini's processing. When instructions are too lengthy, the AI might deprioritize or outright ignore parts of them.

The sweet spot for custom instructions is **500–1,000 characters**. If you need more guidance than that, consider breaking it into multiple Gems, each with a narrower focus, or provide detailed context at the start of each conversation.

**Self-contradictory or competing instructions**

If you tell Gemini "never discuss politics" but also "answer every question comprehensively," you've created a logical conflict. The AI will struggle to honor both, and as a result, may ignore your instructions altogether.

## Common Cause #2: Gem Configuration Issues

Sometimes the problem isn't with your instructions themselves, but with how the Gem is set up. Run through this checklist:

**Your custom instructions aren't actually being saved**

You clicked "Save," but due to a network glitch or browser hiccup, the instructions never actually saved to Gemini's servers. This happens surprisingly often on unstable mobile connections.

How to fix: Reload the page, go back to your Gem's settings, and check if the custom instructions field is empty. If it is, the save failed.

**The Gem's sharing settings are blocking custom instructions**

If you've shared a Gem publicly or with collaborators but haven't enabled the right permissions, those users will interact with a version that doesn't include your custom instructions.

How to fix: Open your Gem's detailed settings and confirm that sharing is set to the correct permission level. Public viewers might need "edit" permissions to see custom instructions applied.

**Browser cache is interfering**

Your browser might be holding onto an older version of your Gem, complete with the previous (or missing) custom instructions. Modern web apps update in real-time, but cached data can override that.

How to fix: Clear your browser cache. In Chrome, press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows) or Command+Shift+Delete (Mac). After clearing, reload your Gem.

## Common Cause #3: Gemini's Built-in Limitations

This is different from a misconfiguration—it's about how Gemini's AI itself works.

**Context length running out**

Every Gemini model has a "context length," which is basically the maximum amount of conversation history it can keep track of. As conversations get longer, older information (including your custom instructions) gradually gets pushed out of the AI's active memory.

This happens especially fast when you're sharing images or long documents repeatedly. The model consumes tokens quickly, and something has to be forgotten.

How to manage: When you're having an important conversation, start a fresh chat occasionally and restate your key instructions. This resets the context and ensures your guidance stays front-and-center.

**Model variation in interpretation**

Even the same instruction might be interpreted slightly differently across different sessions or response generations. This isn't a bug—it's an inherent feature of how large language models work. Perfect consistency isn't possible.

How to manage: Don't expect 100% adherence to every detail. Instead, aim for consistency in spirit. For critical guidance, restate your instructions at the beginning of important conversations.

## Making Custom Instructions Stick: Step-by-Step

Let's walk through the exact process of setting up custom instructions that actually work.

**Step 1: Open your Gem**

From the Google Gemini home screen, click the Gem you want to configure.

**Step 2: Click settings**

Look for a gear icon (⚙️) or three-dot menu in the upper right. Select "Settings."

**Step 3: Find custom instructions**

Scroll down through the settings panel until you see "Custom Instructions." Tap or click it to expand.

**Step 4: Write your instructions**

Compose your instructions in 500–1,000 characters, keeping them clear and free of contradictions. The second half of this guide includes best practices for effective instruction writing.

**Step 5: Save**

Click "Save" or "Apply." You should see a confirmation message within a few seconds.

**Step 6: Test it**

Start a new conversation and check whether your instructions are being followed. A direct test is to ask Gemini: "What are your custom instructions?" and see if it reports them back accurately.

## Best Practices for Effective Custom Instructions

To maximize the likelihood that Gemini will honor your instructions, follow these proven patterns.

**1. Make instructions specific and measurable**

Instead of "be concise," try "keep responses under 150 words." Instead of "explain things clearly," try "define any technical terms in parentheses on first use."

Concrete, countable metrics are far more likely to be followed than abstract ideals.

**2. Use numbered priority when giving multiple instructions**

"1. Always respond in English. 2. Include a summary at the end. 3. List three practical examples." Numbering helps Gemini understand the relative importance and order of your guidance.

**3. Mix positive and negative instruction**

Combine "do this" with "don't do that." For example: "Always use technical language. Never use jargon without explanation." The dual approach is more effective than one-directional guidance.

**4. Revisit and refine regularly**

After using your Gem for a while, you'll notice which instructions are working and which aren't. Edit freely. Custom instructions can be updated at any time.

## Quick Troubleshooting Checklist

When custom instructions aren't working, check these in order:

1. Are the instructions clear, or are they vague/contradictory?
2. Are they under 1,000 characters?
3. Did the save actually complete? (Refresh and check.)
4. Did you clear your browser cache?
5. Is your conversation so long that context is running out?

If any of these ring true, that's likely your culprit. Fix it and test again.

Custom instructions are one of Gemini's best features when used correctly. With the techniques in this guide, you'll be able to build Gems that work exactly the way you want them to. If problems persist, Google's official support team is always available to help.
