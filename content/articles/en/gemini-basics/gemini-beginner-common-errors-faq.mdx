I once sent the same question to Gemini three times in a row. The answer kept stopping mid-sentence, an image wouldn't load, and the Workspace link did nothing at all. I started flipping settings at random, and by the end I couldn't tell which change had actually helped. I redid the whole thing the next morning.

What I'd like to say up front is this: most beginner problems go away if you check the chat and the signed-in account before you touch any setting. Below are seven symptoms, in the order I tend to run into them, with the steps I use to narrow each one down.

:::info
Screen names, plan contents and file limits change with updates. Treat any number here as a rough guide and confirm it in the official Gemini help.
:::

## Q1: Why does the answer stop halfway?

This is the symptom I see most. You ask for something long, and the text just ends mid-sentence.

There are three usual causes:

1. The response hit the maximum length for a single reply.
2. The conversation grew long and its history became heavy.
3. A temporary network or server hiccup.

Here is what I try, from least invasive to most:

- Reply "please continue" and see whether it resumes where it stopped
- Split the request: "just the outline," then "just section one"
- Open a new chat and paste in only the context that matters
- Check your connection and reopen in another browser

Early on I tried to get long text out in one go. It didn't go well — every cut-off made me add another message, and the history only got heavier. These days I split it into three requests from the start: structure, draft, polish. **Hand over long work in pieces, not in one breath.**

:::tip
Telling Gemini the length you want up front, such as "around 800 characters," makes mid-reply cut-offs less frequent.
:::

## Q2: Why does Gemini say it can't process my image?

It should be able to read images, yet sometimes it refuses. There are five broad causes:

1. The format isn't accepted. Converting to JPEG or PNG often fixes it.
2. The file is damaged, or the upload stopped partway.
3. The image is too large. Try shrinking it to a few MB.
4. The content runs into a policy restriction.
5. The browser cache is interfering.

More than once, a photo from my phone was saved in an unusual format and passed after conversion. Try a different image first; if that one works, the problem is on the original's side.

:::warning
Don't upload images that show personal or confidential information.
:::

## Q3: Why do I get English answers to Japanese questions?

Either the account or device display language is English, or the prompt doesn't say which language to use.

- Match the Google account language and the browser language to what you want
- Start the prompt with "Answer everything below in Japanese"
- When pasting English material, add "write the summary in Japanese"

When English came back right after I pasted a long English document, the missing piece was my own instruction. It takes one line, so I keep it as a stock phrase.

## Q4: Why doesn't the Google Workspace link (Gmail, Drive) work?

I check from my own side outward.

1. Which account am I using, work or personal? With several accounts signed in, it's easy to mix them up.
2. Is the integration or extension turned on for that account?
3. Sign out, sign back in, and try in a new chat.
4. For an organization account, has the administrator allowed the feature?

The last item can't be fixed from your end. The quickest route is to ask your admin, "Could you enable the Gemini and Workspace app integration for me?"

:::warning
The Gmail integration is meant for people who use Gmail itself. If you only forward other providers' mail into it, it may not behave as you'd expect.
:::

## Q5: Why does a file upload fail?

I look at three things in order: format, size and count.

- Format: PDFs, text, images and Google Docs are generally fine; executables are not.
- Size: Are you over the limit? For a big PDF, export only the pages you need.
- Count: There is a cap on how many files you can attach at once.

I handed over a thick PDF whole and failed again and again. Cutting out just the chapters I needed went through immediately. Putting the file in Drive and referencing it from there is also a solid option.

:::tip
Get in the habit of glancing over a file for confidential information once before you attach it.
:::

## Q6: Why doesn't an extension take effect after I enable it?

A change you make may not show up in the conversation you're already in.

1. Confirm in settings that the extension really is on.
2. Open a new chat and try again.
3. For anything tied to an outside service, disconnect it and authorize again.
4. Clear the browser cache and try another browser too.

An expired authorization was the most common cause in my case.

## Q7: I'm on a paid plan, but features are missing. Where do I look?

Plan names and contents have been reshuffled several times, so use your own subscription screen as the reference, not the name written in an article.

1. Check your current plan and payment status in your Google account's management page.
2. Are you signed in to Gemini with the account that made the purchase? Mix-ups between accounts are common.
3. Right after purchase, it can take a little while to apply.
4. Is your region, age or organization account outside the supported range?
5. Try a normal window with extensions and privacy settings temporarily off.

Whenever I felt "I paid but can't use it," I was signed in with a different account. Before doubting the subscription, look at the email address behind the icon at the top right.

## Keep the order, not the details

The order shared by all seven symptoms:

1. Resend the same question in a new chat.
2. Check which account you're signed in with.
3. Try another browser or another device.
4. Look at settings, permissions and the plan.
5. If it still fails, tell the official help or support exactly what you saw.

**Before touching settings, try the same thing somewhere else first.** Since I started keeping to that line, hunts that used to eat half a day finish in about ten minutes.

Next time something feels off, try resending the same question once in a new chat before you open the settings screen.
