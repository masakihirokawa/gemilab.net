## #2: Apple Vision Framework × Gemini API — Cutting Wallpaper Cloud Inference Costs by 70%

[Apple Vision Framework × Gemini API: Hybrid Image Recognition — Cutting Wallpaper App Cloud Inference Costs by 70%](/articles/gemini-api/gemini-vision-apple-vision-hybrid-cost-reduction-wallpaper)

In my wallpaper apps, the preview pipeline runs light image analysis on user images — flagging frames where skin dominates the composition, tagging images with heavy text, and so on. Sending all of it through Gemini Vision gave great accuracy, but as usage rose, cloud inference cost overran the budget.

I switched to a two-stage flow: Apple Vision Framework first determines whatever it can confidently classify on-device, and only the uncertain cases get handed off to Gemini Vision. Monthly cloud inference cost dropped by about 70%. The article walks through where I draw the line between "Vision can be trusted" and "this needs Gemini," with implementation samples.

## #3: Running Gemini 3 Pro and Flash in Parallel on AdMob Monthly Reports

[Running Gemini 3 Pro and Flash in Parallel on AdMob Monthly Reports — Notes from Ten Mornings](/articles/gemini-api/gemini-3-pro-flash-admob-monthly-report-parallel-memo)

Ten days of pushing my AdMob monthly report through Gemini 3 Pro and Flash in parallel, asking each the same questions, surfaced a useful split. Pro is visibly sharper at "what should I try next, given the prior month's context"; Flash is fast enough at "give me the overview and flag the outliers."

If you also watch AdMob revenue for indie titles, you've probably circled the same problem. Leaving the report open as a pinned tab doesn't make decisions happen; running Pro and Flash side-by-side does. The article includes a thin orchestration layer that fires off both models with the same payload.

## #5: Three Months of Running Help Translation on Gemini 2.5 Flash

[Running In-App Help Translation on Gemini 2.5 Flash for Three Months — An Indie Developer's Notes](/articles/gemini-api/gemini-flash-iap-help-translation-3month-indie-review)

My wallpaper apps now ship in English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, and Spanish. After switching the help-document translation pipeline to Gemini 2.5 Flash three months ago, "the translation feels off" reviews almost disappeared.

The key wasn't translating mechanically — it was committing to passing Flash a per-call brief: the app's world, the target audience, the phrasing to avoid. Cost-wise, Flash is dramatically lower than Pro, which makes it the right pragmatic default for indie multilingual support.
